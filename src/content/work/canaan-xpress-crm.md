## Context

Canaan Xpress had never had a CRM. Sales tracking, customer segmentation, and outreach had no shared data foundation.

## My role

I am a B2B marketing analyst on contract. I led the CRM implementation and own the analysis, reporting, and marketing channels built on top of it.

## What I did

- Led the implementation of Maximizer, the company's first CRM, creating the data foundation for sales tracking, customer segmentation, and automated outreach to 500+ target companies.
- Queried the CRM data with SQL to tier repeat customers by order frequency and volume.
- Analyzed Q2 performance and turned the findings into a customer growth guide that focuses sales on retention.
- Built a dashboard, and the APIs connecting it to CRM data, so leadership gets visual reports on customer trends.
- Launched SEO, Google Business Profile, and LinkedIn channels, and wrote SOPs so ongoing operations can be handed off with little maintenance.

## Results

- Q2 analysis showed 90% retention and 10% growth, which shaped the retention focus of the growth guide.
- Leadership has visual reports on customer trends, drawn from CRM data.
- 500+ target companies are in place for automated outreach.
