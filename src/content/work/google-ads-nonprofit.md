## Context

A nonprofit client was advertising through a Google Ads Grant, which comes with compliance restrictions. The campaigns also suffered from algorithmic data starvation: the bidding algorithm did not have enough conversion data to work with.

## My role

As a digital marketing extern, I strategized and managed the end-to-end launch of the campaigns and presented strategy to the nonprofit's stakeholders.

## What I did

- Launched 4 net-new live Google Ads campaigns across Search and Performance Max, working within the Google Ads Grant compliance restrictions.
- Implemented a top-of-funnel "Page View" conversion goal to resolve the data starvation.
- Ran ongoing Search Query Analyses and built data visualizations.
- Delivered formal, data-driven strategy presentations to the nonprofit's stakeholders.

## Results

- Impressions rose more than 120% day over day after the conversion goal went in.
- Total clicks rose 50%.
