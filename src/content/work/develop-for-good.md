## Context

Linens N Love needed a donation management platform that connects volunteers, hotels, and shelters, with administrators overseeing the handling between them.

## My role

I held two roles at Develop For Good, one after the other. From May to September 2024 I was a software engineer intern building the Linens N Love MVP. From October 2024 to March 2025 I was a product manager intern, working with nonprofit stakeholders and directing a team of 8 engineers.

## What I did

**As an engineer**

- Delivered the MVP as a full-stack MERN application, from 0 to launch.
- Led weekly client discovery and feedback sessions to scope the MVP requirements.
- Built RESTful APIs with Node.js and Express, and managed database operations in MongoDB, so data moved efficiently and securely between volunteers, hotels, shelters, and administrators.

**As a product manager**

- Gathered requirements from nonprofit stakeholders and translated their feedback into wireframes for a landing page redesign.
- Directed a team of 8 engineers integrating PowerBI reporting and print-on-demand, owning the technical scoping and tracking implementation through launch.
- Monitored post-launch engagement and utilization to define the product roadmap.

## Results

- The MVP launched, with launch milestones hit on time.
- The PowerBI reporting and print-on-demand integrations were tracked through to launch.
- The roadmap prioritized features to improve retention and drive adoption beyond the MVP.
